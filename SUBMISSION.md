# org-id.guide submission — GB-POL

Draft PROPOSAL for adding the `GB-POL` list to org-id.guide. Submit as a PROPOSAL issue (or PR) on the org-id.guide register on GitHub, under a GitHub account, once this repo is public.

## Metadata

- **List code:** `GB-POL`
- **Name:** UK Police Forces (RCVDA register)
- **Description:** Canonical identifiers for UK police forces (the 43 England & Wales territorial forces, Police Scotland, PSNI, and UK-wide non-territorial forces). RCVDA mints a code where a force has no usable existing identifier; forces that already hold one are referenced to it.
- **Geographic coverage:** GB (ISO 3166 — the United Kingdom, incl. Northern Ireland)
- **Legal structure:** Statutory body / corporation sole
- **Sector:** Policing
- **List type:** Third-party
- **Available online:** https://github.com/rcvda/police-force-names-and-codes (data: `data/register.csv`)
- **How to locate identifiers:** Look up the force by name/area in `data/register.csv`; the `org_id` column holds the `GB-POL` code.
- **Example identifiers:** `GB-POL-00006` (Cleveland Police), `GB-POL-00001` (Avon and Somerset)
- **Open licence:** yes — Open Government Licence v3.0
- **Last updated:** 2026-11-08

## Verified example

`GB-POL-00006` — Cleveland Police. Minted by RCVDA; also recorded in RCVDA's CRM. Cleveland Police has no charity number, company number, or government-organisation (GB-GOR) code — a genuine identifier gap.
